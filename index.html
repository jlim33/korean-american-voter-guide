Korean Ballot Legal Rights (§203)

Federal Voting Rights Act

Under federal law, counties with sizable Korean-speaking populations must provide ballots, sample voting guides, and election workers fluent in Korean.

  • California: Los Angeles County, Orange County
  • New York: Queens County (New York City)
  • New Jersey: Bergen County
  • Georgia: Gwinnett County (Metro Atlanta)
  • Virginia: Fairfax County (Metro Washington DC)
  • Illinois: Cook County (Chicago area)
Voter Tip: You can legally bring any bilingual family member or friend into the booth to translate for you, as long as they are not your boss or union leader.

Mail-In & Absentee Ballots

Vote from Home

Many states like California automatically send ballots by mail to all active registered voters, while other states require requesting an absentee ballot ahead of time.

  • Sign your envelope: Always match the signature on your Driver's License/ID.
  • Postmark deadline: Mail your ballot several days before election day or drop it in an official county drop-box.
  • Track your ballot: Use tools like "Where's My Ballot" in CA/CO/WA to receive SMS alerts when your vote is counted.
